I hold a Principal Software Engineer (and Member of Technical Staff) position at Sony Depthsensing Solutions, where I work in the AI & Perception team on machine learning-based solutions to imaging and 3D sensing problems.

Since my Ph.D., my research focuses on computational imaging, i.e., the joint design of sensing systems and algorithms to achieve crisp, accurate, noise-free image and 3D scene reconstruction from few, noisy or indirect measurements, within the memory and compute budgets of real devices. My recent public-domain works revolve on depth estimation from either very sparse time-of-flight measurements, low frame rate active sensors, or monocular depth estimation; I also contributed to some research on 3D/4D Gaussian splatting. My earlier Ph.D. and postdoc research developed extensions of compressed sensing with applications to encryption at the physical layer, quantized random embeddings, blind sensor calibration and compressive multispectral imaging.
