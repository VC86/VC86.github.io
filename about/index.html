about

My research interest is computational imaging: building imaging systems in which the sensor and the reconstruction algorithm are designed together, within the memory and compute budgets of real devices.

I joined Sony Depthsensing Solutions in Brussels in 2017 as a time-of-flight camera system engineer. Since then I have developed real-time algorithms for depth map restoration and RGB-D sensor fusion, led the product-level RGB-D Fusion software library, and moved on to learning-based depth estimation and 3D reconstruction. I also lead our collaborations with university labs, co-advising Ph.D. students at the CVLAB of the University of Bologna. In 2024 I became Principal Software Engineer and was elected Member of Technical Staff.

Before Sony, I was a postdoctoral researcher with Prof. Laurent Jacques in the Image and Signal Processing Group at UCLouvain, supported by an F.R.S.-FNRS fellowship, working on non-convex blind calibration and quantized random embeddings. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Bologna in 2015, advised by Prof. Riccardo Rovatti and Prof. Gianluca Setti, with a thesis on matrix designs for secure and efficient compressed sensing, for which I was awarded the title of Doctor Europaeus and the 2020 EURASIP Best PhD Award.
