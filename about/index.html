about

My general research interest is computational imaging. I like problems where the sensor and the algorithm have to be designed together, and where the answer must fit in the memory and compute of a real device.

I joined Sony Depthsensing Solutions in Brussels in 2017 as a time-of-flight camera system engineer, and have since worked on depth restoration, RGB-D fusion, learned depth estimation and 3D reconstruction, leading collaborations with university labs along the way. In 2024 I was elected Member of Technical Staff.

Before that I was a postdoctoral researcher with Prof. Laurent Jacques at UCLouvain, working on non-convex blind calibration and quantized random embeddings, and a Ph.D. student with Prof. Riccardo Rovatti and Prof. Gianluca Setti at the University of Bologna, where my thesis was on matrix designs for secure and efficient compressed sensing.

I am also an avid Magic: The Gathering player since 1996.
